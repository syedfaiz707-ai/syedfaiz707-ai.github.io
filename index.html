Featured · Product I built and own

An HR & payroll SaaS, built with AI in about 6 months

As HR Head I needed better tools than the paid HR system we were using, so I built them myself. I wrote the requirements and directed AI (Claude Code) through design, build, testing and deployment. The platform now runs on the company's own server.

Payroll & statutory engine

PF, ESI, Professional Tax, LWF and income tax (TDS), plus arrears, loans, overtime, NPS and approval-gated salary revisions. Checked against real salary statements.

Attendance & rosters

Biometric swipe sync, shift rosters, auto-calculated attendance, overtime, and month-end finalisation that turns absences into loss-of-pay.

Leave management

Eight leave types with quotas, carry-forward rules, comp-off, holidays and balances, all feeding straight into payroll.

One approval console

Leave, regularisation, overtime, resignation, reimbursement, confirmation, help desk and more, with multi-level approvals and delegates.

Employee portal

Payslips, tax planner, proof of investment, documents, org chart, company feed, self-onboarding and push notifications on mobile.

Reports & analytics

A 200-report gallery (payroll, statutory registers, attendance, attrition, MIS) and an employee filter with 205 fields.

Biometric swipes→Attendance→Leave & LOP→Payroll→Payslips & statutory reports

Built on Next.js, React and PostgreSQL. Automated cloud builds, a database backup before every update, automatic rollback and uptime alerts. Demo available on request.
